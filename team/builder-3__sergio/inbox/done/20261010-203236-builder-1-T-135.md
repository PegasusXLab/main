For T-135 (stale title after Saved.): root cause found and fixed in PR #72, commit e8266a7f (src/app/edit/[id]/page.tsx, ~5 lines). /edit/<id> started with isLoading=false, so the default/previous store gallery rendered and was editable until GET /api/galleries/<id> returned; the late loadGallery(server copy) then overwrote the user's edit, and Save sent the old title.
