## Builder notes
### What changed
Items opt in to inspection (an "Inspectable" switch plus details table, link, story and audio guide in the artwork/object panels). A new dark side-panel inspection view (bottom sheet on phones) replaces the white plaque, and the camera reframes the piece beside it. A hover cue (aura, outline/halo, "Inspect" label, pointer) appears on inspectable pieces only. Room and item narration play once per visit and can be replayed ("Listen to the room guide" chip, panel audio button). Titles wrap between words. The editor's collapsed Panel tab moved to mid-left. The redundant Song name field is removed (#122 merged before that commit).
### How to test
1. Editor → select a room → + Add → Picture: the Inspectable switch is on; add Year/Medium chips, fill values, add an https link, save.
2. Publish and open `/g/<id>`: hover the piece (pointer, aura, "Inspect" label). Click it: the panel slides in, rows animate, the guide plays once. Esc closes it. Open it again: no auto-play; the play button replays.
3. Turn Inspectable off on an artwork: no cue, and clicking does nothing in the tour.
4. Phone width: the panel is a bottom sheet with the piece framed above it.
5. Editor: collapse the side panel; the vertical "Panel" tab sits mid-left, clear of the gallery name.
