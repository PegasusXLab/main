# Handoff: PR-77 / T-127
## Builder notes (designer)
### What changed
- **Neutral tone mapping**: creators' wall hexes render faithfully (`Scene.tsx` `onCreated`).
- **IBL**: `SceneEnvironment` (RoomEnvironment PMREM, once per canvas). Its intensity comes from the pure `lookExtras(look)` in `atmosphere.ts`, so it blends with the existing 1 s atmosphere blend. The hemisphere light is now a 45% fill.
- **Picture lights**: a fixed pool (8 high / 4 low) in `PictureLights.tsx`, re-aimed per room with a 1 s fade-up. Pure `aimPictureLights()` is unit-tested.
- **Ceilings** (`Ceilings.tsx`): FrontSide planes facing down, invisible from above.
- **Walls**: baseboards merged into geometry (`baseboardBox`) plus floor-junction AO (`injectWallAO`).
- **Floors**: procedural wood/concrete/marble plus edge AO (`injectFloorLook`). This replaces the spec's CC0 textures (no download, no licence work).
- **Frames**: moulding depth, a mat and a soft shadow (`frameMatWidth`, `framedSize`, `MAT_Z`, `ART_Z`).
- **Quality tier**: `detectQuality()`, overridable with `?quality=low|high`.

- Phone FPS isn't measured on real hardware (headless uses software GL). Please spot-check on the preview.
