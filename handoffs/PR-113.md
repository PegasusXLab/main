### What changed

### How to test
`/edit/local`:
- Drag the empty grid: it pans. Click the grid: it still adds a room.
- Pinch on a trackpad: smooth. Two-finger scroll: pans.
- Select a room and press A: a room appears next to it. Press Backspace: it's deleted (Cmd+Z restores).
- Right-click a room: the menu appears.
- Live 3D Preview → Split: both halves show the whole plan.
