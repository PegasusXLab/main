# PR-52: BUGFIX minimap hides sound controls in viewer (T-082)

## Summary
- Repositions the `<aside>` minimap wrapper in `src/app/g/[id]/page.tsx` from `top-4 right-4` to `top-16 sm:top-20 right-4`.
