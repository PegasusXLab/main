- `schema/types.ts`: optional `Gallery.objects` / `Gallery.look`, optional `Room.look` / `wallFinish` / `titleFont`, `GalleryObject`, `ObjectSource`, `GenerationJob` (spec). `Artwork` untouched; `version` stays 1.
